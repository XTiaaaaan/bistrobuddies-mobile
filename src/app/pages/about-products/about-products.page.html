How pricing works

Prices shown in the app are for reference only. When you place an order, the BistroBuddies backend recalculates the subtotal, delivery fee and total from its own product records, so the amount you pay is never taken from your device.

If a drink is sold out or removed from the menu, the cart and checkout will ask you to update your order before it can be submitted.
